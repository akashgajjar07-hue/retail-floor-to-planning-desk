## LinkedIn post ideas (2–3 over a few weeks)

**Post 1: Sales are not demand**
> On the shop floor I saw it every week: a rail with only XS and XXL left. The customer who wanted an M walks out, and that sale never appears in the data.
>
> I built a simulation to measure what that does to forecasting. On lines that were out of stock 15–30% of the time, a forecast from sales underestimated real demand by 25%. Using only days that ended with stock cut the error to 6%.
>

**Post 2: A T-shirt and a jacket shouldn't be planned the same way**
> Basics are reordered all year, so a reorder point works. A seasonal jacket is bought once, months ahead. Its real decisions are transfer, mark down or hold. Here's how I split the logic, and what happened to summer leftovers (1,933 units down to 246 in the simulation).

**Post 3: What I'd change at a real retailer**
> Synthetic data has limits. Here's what this model leaves out (case packs, DC stock, display minimums) and how I'd roll it out with planners approving every order.
